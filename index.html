I've spent my undergrad breaking into machines, building SIEM pipelines, and training detectors that looked great until they met new traffic. That last part is what I want to research. Each dot on this page is a packet. The orange ones don't belong.

01
Research question · Learned detection

Can intrusion detection generalize past its training data?

I built ML intrusion detectors on KDD99 and watched them score well on attacks they had already seen. What I want to study is why they fail quietly on new traffic, and how to make them fail loudly.

02
Research question · Detection engineering

How do detections survive attackers who adapt?

Hunting through Windows event logs with Chainsaw and Sigma showed me how brittle signature rules are. I want to measure rule coverage and evasion, and test detection logic the way we test code.

SigmaSIEMDFIRLog analytics

Behind this: one event chain, tangled the way real intrusions are

03
Research question · Secure by design

Can threat modeling be measured, not just practiced?

At Syntricx I ran STRIDE reviews and wired Checkmarx, Burp Suite, and Snyk into CI/CD. Each one caught different things. I want to find where design-time analysis and code scanning disagree, and what that gap costs.
